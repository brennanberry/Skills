# Real Estate: Acquisition Memo

Produce investment committee-ready acquisition memos for industrial real estate deals. Use for IC approvals, LP co-investor updates, acquisition rationale documentation, and pre-LOI deal evaluation.

## Inputs Required
- Property specs (address, SF, asset type, condition)
- Pricing and seller/broker context
- Rent roll, occupancy, and operating expense history
- Financing terms
- Comparable market data (sales and leases)
- Fund strategy alignment

## Memo Structure

1. **Executive Summary** — One-page overview: property details, strategy type, target returns, recommendation
2. **Property Overview** — Physical characteristics, condition, functional considerations
3. **Market Analysis** — Vacancy, absorption, rent data, comp transactions and leases
4. **Tenancy & Lease Analysis** — Tenant roster, credit quality, lease expiration risk
5. **Financial Underwriting** — Sources/uses, NOI projections, return metrics, sensitivity analysis
6. **Value Creation Plan** — Specific initiatives with timelines and cost estimates
7. **Risk Factors** — Deal-specific risks with mitigation strategies

## Quality Standards
- Returns must be calculated, not estimated
- Market data must be sourced with dates
- Risks must be specific, not generic
- Recommendation must be stated without ambiguity

# Deep Research

Systematic research protocol for industrial real estate — markets, tenants, competitors, and regulatory matters.

## Three-Step Framework

**Step 1: Define the Research Question**
- Specify geography, timeframe, and subject
- Types: market outlook, tenant creditworthiness, zoning/entitlement, competitive positioning, environmental

**Step 2: Source Selection by Research Type**

| Research Type | Primary Sources |
|--------------|----------------|
| Market data | CoStar, major brokerage reports, local broker networks |
| Tenant analysis | SEC filings, D&B, credit agencies, news |
| Regulatory | Planning departments, EPA databases, legal counsel |
| Competitor | SEC filings, press, professional networks |

**Step 3: Structured Report Output**
1. Executive summary (key finding + confidence level)
2. Background and scope
3. Themed sections with data tables
4. Risk assessment
5. Conclusions and implications
6. Source citations with dates

## Research Standards
- Cite sources for every material fact
- Date all data — flag anything older than 12 months
- Acknowledge conflicting information; do not omit it
- Separate factual findings from analytical interpretation
- Quantify uncertainty ranges explicitly

## Scope Coverage
Industrial submarket analysis, tenant financial viability, environmental/zoning compliance, investment thesis development, industry sector trends

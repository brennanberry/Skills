# Sales: Call Preparation

Prepare for LP meetings, tenant negotiations, and broker calls — background research, talking points, and questions. Role: Asset Manager/Fund Manager.

## Four-Step Prep Process

1. **Counterparty Research**
   - Review prior interactions and open items
   - Recent news about their firm or portfolio
   - Their current interests, concerns, and priorities

2. **Position & Messages**
   - Your stance on key likely topics
   - Supporting data for each position
   - What you need from this call

3. **Q&A Preparation**
   - Anticipate the 3–5 hardest questions
   - Prepare honest, data-backed responses (not spin)

4. **Call Agenda**
   - Opening (rapport / context): 2 min
   - Main topics with time allocation
   - Decision or next step: last 5 min

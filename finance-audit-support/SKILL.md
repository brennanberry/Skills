# Finance: Audit Support

Prepare audit materials for industrial real estate entities. Covers LP/investor audits, lender compliance reviews, and external financial statement audits. Role: Controller. Frequency: Monthly.

## PBC (Prepared by Client) Framework

Standard document categories:
1. Financial statements (draft and final)
2. Bank statements and reconciliations
3. Rent roll and lease files
4. AP/AR aging schedules
5. Fixed asset schedules and depreciation
6. Loan documents and compliance certificates
7. Organizational documents and entity agreements
8. Related-party transaction support
9. Legal correspondence
10. Prior audit workpapers and adjusting entries

Use a tracker: Item | Assignee | Due Date | Status

## Documentation Standards
Each schedule shows: GL balance → reconciling items → supporting docs with file references.
Every schedule requires preparer and reviewer sign-off with dates.

## Communication Protocol
- Acknowledge auditor requests within 24 hours
- Provide status updates on outstanding items
- Flag items needing more time with completion estimates
- Controller reviews all draft documents before release

## Common Response Areas
- **Revenue**: Lease compliance and CAM reconciliation support
- **Accrued liabilities**: Invoice-based and contract-based documentation
- **Related parties**: Affiliate disclosures and executed agreements

# Real Estate: NOI & Returns Analysis

Calculate net operating income, cap rate, IRR, cash-on-cash return, and DSCR for industrial assets. Use for acquisition decisions, portfolio reviews, and hold-versus-sell evaluations.

## Inputs Required
- Rent levels and vacancy assumptions
- Operating expenses (taxes, insurance, utilities, maintenance, mgmt fees)
- Purchase price and loan terms (rate, amortization, LTV)
- Equity invested
- Holding period and exit cap rate (for IRR)

## Metrics Calculated

| Metric | Formula | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| NOI | Gross Revenue − Vacancy − OpEx | — |
| Cap Rate | NOI ÷ Purchase Price | 5.5–7.5% industrial |
| Cash-on-Cash | Annual Cash Flow ÷ Equity Invested | — |
| DSCR | NOI ÷ Annual Debt Service | Min 1.20x (lender req) |
| IRR | Equity cash flows over hold period | 12–18% value-add target |

## Risk Flags
- DSCR below 1.20x → insufficient debt coverage
- Exit cap rate compression → valuation risk
- Vacancy assumptions below market → overly optimistic underwriting

## Output Format
Present a summary table with all five metrics, assumptions page, and a 3-point risk commentary.

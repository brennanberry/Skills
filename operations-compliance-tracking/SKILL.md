# Operations: Compliance Tracking

Monitor regulatory, lease, and reporting obligations for industrial real estate portfolios. Role: Asset Manager/Operations. Frequency: Weekly.

## Master Register Fields
`ID | Type | Property | Frequency | Due Date | Owner | Status | Notes`

## Status Indicators
- 🟢 Complete
- 🟡 In progress
- 🟠 Due within 14 days
- 🔴 Overdue
- ⚪ Scheduled (> 30 days out)

## Four Compliance Categories

**1. Lease Obligations**
- Repair and maintenance duties
- CAM reconciliations (annual deadline)
- Estoppel certificates
- Rent escalation notices

**2. Regulatory Requirements**
- Environmental monitoring (frequency per permit)
- Fire/sprinkler inspections
- Elevator certifications
- Business permits and licenses

**3. REIT Compliance**
- Quarterly asset tests
- Annual income tests
- Distribution requirements (90% of taxable income)

**4. Administrative**
- Insurance certificate renewals
- Business license renewals
- Lender reporting requirements

## Tiered Alert Protocol
- **30 days out**: Assign owner, confirm requirements, begin prep
- **14 days out**: Confirm progress, escalate if not started
- **7 days out**: Final confirmation, escalate to Asset Manager
- **Overdue**: Immediate AM intervention, assess penalty exposure

## Output
90-day rolling compliance register with owner assignments and status indicators.

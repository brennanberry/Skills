# Marketing: Competitive Brief

Build competitive market briefs for fund positioning, asset marketing, and leasing campaigns. Audiences: LPs, tenants, brokers.

## Use Cases
- Fund marketing collateral and LP pitch context
- Property leasing materials and broker tour packages
- Investor pitch competitive positioning
- Board presentations

## Brief Structure
1. Executive summary positioning statement
2. Market metrics (vacancy, absorption, asking rents, supply pipeline)
3. Competitive factor analysis vs. comparable assets/funds
4. Win/loss competitive positioning
5. Audience-specific core narrative (see below)
6. Supporting proof points (specific deals, metrics, testimonials)
7. Tiered messaging (short: 1 sentence / medium: 1 paragraph / long: full narrative)

## Audience-Specific Angles

**LPs**
- Strategy differentiation
- Risk-adjusted returns vs. benchmarks
- Team experience and track record

**Tenants**
- Building specifications (clear height, dock doors, power)
- Location advantages (labor, logistics, access)
- Landlord responsiveness and TI flexibility
- Total occupancy cost vs. alternatives

**Brokers**
- Commission terms and co-broke track record
- Space specs and availability
- Decision-making speed and flexibility

## Deliverable
Completed brief with: sourced market data table, competitive comparison matrix, differentiation points (3–5), and audience-ready messaging copy.

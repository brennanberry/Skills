# Real Estate: Fund LP Report

Generate professional, investor-grade quarterly LP reports for industrial real estate funds.

## Inputs Required
- Fund name, quarter/year
- NAV, equity deployed, YTD distributions
- Asset-level metrics (occupancy, NOI, leasing activity, capex progress)
- Capital activity (acquisitions, dispositions, refinancings)
- Market commentary points

## Report Structure

1. **Fund Overview** — Highlights and performance summary table (NAV, IRR, distributions, occupancy)
2. **Portfolio Update** — Per-asset: occupancy, NOI, leasing activity, capex progress
3. **Capital Activity** — Acquisitions, dispositions, and refinancings completed
4. **Market Commentary** — Regional supply/demand and rent trend analysis
5. **Distributions & Capital** — Declaration and cumulative distribution tracking
6. **Outlook** — Near-term priorities and risk monitoring

## Standards
- Use exact figures ("$2.1M NOI"), never qualitative placeholders
- Disclose material issues clearly — do not minimize
- Verify all numbers before inclusion; IRR and multiples must align with capital account calculations
- Target length: 4–6 pages including tables
- Tone: professional but accessible, lead with quantitative performance data

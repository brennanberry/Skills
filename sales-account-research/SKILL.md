# Sales: Account Research

Research prospective tenants, LP investors, and JV partners for industrial real estate. Role: Asset Manager/Fund Manager. Frequency: Weekly.

## Tenant Research
**Sources**: D&B credit report, public financials (SEC EDGAR for public cos), news search, LinkedIn, state business registry

**Output**:
- Company overview (HQ, size, industry, industrial footprint)
- Credit rating or proxy assessment
- Industrial real estate requirements (SF, markets, lease term)
- Risk factors (financial distress signals, lease concentration, industry headwinds)
- **Recommendation**: Proceed / Proceed with conditions / Decline

## LP / Investor Research
**Sources**: Firm website, ADV filing (for RIAs), news, LinkedIn, prior co-investors

**Assess**:
- Firm type (family office, RIA, endowment, fund of funds)
- AUM and industrial RE allocation target
- Key decision-makers and investment committee process
- Check size and preferred structure (direct, fund, co-invest)
- Return expectations and hold period preference
- **Fit assessment**: Strong / Moderate / Weak vs. your fund

## JV Partner Research
**Assess**:
- Track record in industrial deals (deal count, markets, SF)
- Financial capacity and balance sheet
- Operational strengths (development, property management, leasing)
- Market reputation (broker and lender references)
- Fee structure and alignment

## Output Format
One-page profile per prospect: overview, key data points, risk factors, fit assessment, recommended next step.

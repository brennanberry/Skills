# Operations: Capacity Planning

Manage capacity across industrial real estate portfolios. Role: Asset Manager/Operations. Frequency: Monthly.

## Four Planning Dimensions

**1. Space & Leasing Capacity**
- Track occupancy, available SF, and leasing pipeline velocity
- Calculate "months of supply" = Available SF ÷ Monthly Absorption Rate
- Flag if pipeline < 2 months of projected leasing need

**2. Team Capacity**
- Monitor workload by role: Asset Manager, Property Manager, Controller
- Flag roles exceeding 80% utilization as bottleneck risk
- Track: properties per AM, open work orders per PM, entities per Controller

**3. CapEx Pipeline**
- Schedule projects; check for overlapping timelines
- Confirm vendor availability before committing start dates
- Align with committed capital and draw schedule

**4. Acquisition Pipeline**
- Assess organizational readiness before adding assets
- Check: underwriting bandwidth, PM absorption capacity, legal capacity

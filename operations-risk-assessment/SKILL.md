# Operations: Risk Assessment

Systematic evaluation of industrial real estate risks. Use for quarterly reviews, acquisition due diligence, CapEx projects, portfolio reporting, insurance renewals, and regulatory compliance. Role: Asset Manager/Fund Manager. Frequency: Monthly.

## Eight Risk Categories
1. Physical / property condition
2. Environmental
3. Leasing / occupancy
4. Financial
5. Market
6. Regulatory
7. Operational
8. Natural hazard

## Scoring Methodology
`Risk Score = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5)`

| Score Range | Rating |
|------------|--------|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## Four Mitigation Approaches
- **Avoid** — eliminate the activity or exposure
- **Mitigate** — reduce likelihood or impact
- **Transfer** — insurance, contract indemnification
- **Accept** — document decision and monitor

## Risk Register Format
`ID | Category | Risk Description | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Rating | Mitigation | Owner | Due Date | Status`

## Deliverables
1. Prioritized risk register (sorted by score, descending)
2. Heat map (likelihood vs. impact matrix)
3. Mitigation action plan with owners and deadlines
4. Executive summary paragraph (3–5 sentences for IC/LP reporting)

# Operations: Vendor Review

Systematic vendor evaluation for industrial real estate contractors and service providers.

## Five-Step Process

1. **Gather credentials** — company background, licensing, insurance certificates, references, pricing, service capacity
2. **Score using evaluation matrix**
3. **Review contract terms** for risk issues
4. **Check references** using structured questions
5. **Document recommendation**

## Evaluation Criteria & Weighting

| Criterion | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Price competitiveness | 25% |
| Relevant experience | 20% |
| Licensing & insurance | 20% |
| References / reputation | 15% |
| Capacity / availability | 10% |
| Communication quality | 10% |

## Contract Risk Flags
- No cap on liability
- Auto-renewal clauses without notice window
- Missing indemnification language
- Unfavorable payment schedules (large upfront, no holdback)
- Exclusivity restrictions

## Reference Check Questions (5)
1. How long have you worked with this vendor and what was the scope?
2. Did they complete work on time and within budget?
3. How did they handle problems when they arose?
4. How responsive were they to communication?
5. Would you hire them again?

## Automatic Disqualifiers
- No general liability insurance
- Missing required licenses
- No fixed business address
- Unresolved negative reviews
- Refusal to provide references

## Recommendation Memo Format
Property | Service Type | Selected Vendor | Rationale | Contract Value & Term | Identified Risks | Next Steps + Owner

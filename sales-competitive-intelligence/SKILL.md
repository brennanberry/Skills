# Sales: Competitive Intelligence

Research and analyze competitors in industrial real estate markets. Use for acquisition underwriting, investor positioning, tenant alternative space analysis, and pricing benchmarks. Role: Asset Manager/Analyst. Frequency: Monthly.

## Four-Step Research Process

**Step 1: Build Competitive Set**
Compare your asset vs. comps across:
`SF | Clear Height | Dock Doors | Year Built | Owner | Asking Rent ($/SF) | Occupancy | Recent Lease Activity`

**Step 2: Market Positioning Analysis**
- What do tenants choose over your property and why?
- Competitor concession packages (free rent, TI allowances)
- Your asset's relative strengths and weaknesses

**Step 3: Competing Fund / Operator Analysis**
`AUM | Strategy | Target Markets | Recent Deals | LP Base | Track Record | Fee Structure`

**Step 4: Synthesis Brief**
1. Executive summary (your positioning in 2–3 sentences)
2. Comp analysis table
3. Market conditions summary
4. Competitive positioning (strengths / weaknesses / opportunities / threats)
5. Strategic implications and recommended actions
6. Sources with dates

# Sales: Call Summary

Document sales and relationship calls with key points, decisions, and next steps. Use for LP meetings, tenant interactions, broker discussions, and lender conversations. Role: Asset Manager/Fund Manager. Frequency: Daily.

## Summary Template
```
CALL SUMMARY
Date:         [YYYY-MM-DD]
Duration:     [X min]
Attendees:    [Name, Role — Name, Role]
Context:      [One sentence: purpose/relationship stage]

DISCUSSION POINTS
1. [Factual summary — what was said, not interpreted]
2. [Factual summary]
3. [Factual summary]

DECISIONS MADE
-
-

OPEN QUESTIONS
-
-

ACTION ITEMS
| Action | Owner | Due Date |
|--------|-------|----------|
| [Task] | [Name] | [Date] |

RELATIONSHIP NOTE (if relevant)
[Sentiment shifts, concerns raised, opportunities flagged]
```

## Three Deliverables Per Call
1. **Full summary** using template above
2. **CRM entry** (2–3 lines): Date | Key point | Next action
3. **Follow-up email draft** (if commitments were made)

## Tone Standards
- Factual, not interpretive
- Specific commitments (not general impressions)
- Use exact numbers when discussed
- Note relationship sentiment shifts where relevant

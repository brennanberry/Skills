# Sales: Draft Outreach

Write personalized outreach emails for industrial real estate professionals targeting LPs, tenants, and brokers.

## Core Rules
- Lead with value or insight — not a pitch
- Cold outreach: under 150 words
- One clear ask per email
- Research-backed and specific (not generic)

## Pre-Outreach Research
Before writing, identify:
- Recent news about the prospect's firm
- Mutual connections
- Their investment/space focus
- Relevant deals in your market

## Templates by Recipient

**LP Prospect** (family office, RIA, endowment)
> Subject: Industrial RE — [X]% cash yield, [market] portfolio
> [Reference their focus or a mutual connection]. We manage [X SF] of industrial assets in [market] — current portfolio is generating [X]% cash-on-cash with [occupancy]% occupancy. We have a [co-invest / fund opening] that may fit your allocation. Available for 20 minutes this [week/month]?

**Tenant Outreach** (direct industrial occupiers)
> Subject: [SF] available — [address/market], [spec relevant to their ops]
> [Company]-sized space at [address]: [SF], [clear height], [dock count], available [date]. [One specific reason it fits their operation]. Happy to send specs or schedule a tour.

**Broker**
> Subject: Co-broke — [SF], [address], [commission]
> [Property]: [SF] available [date]. [X]% co-broke. [One key spec]. Can send OM and add to your tour list — who's your contact for [submarket]?

## Follow-Up Sequence
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Initial email |
| 5 | Brief follow-up (1–2 sentences) |
| 14 | Market insight follow-up |
| 30+ | Quarterly nurture |

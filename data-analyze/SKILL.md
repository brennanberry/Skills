# Data: Analyze

Examine structured datasets and extract actionable insights for industrial real estate portfolios. Use for rent roll reviews, expense analysis, collections tracking, and tenant concentration assessments. Role: Analyst/Controller. Frequency: Daily.

## Six-Step Analysis Framework

1. **Data validation** — understand column definitions, time periods, data quality issues
2. **Descriptive metrics** — totals, averages, top/bottom performers
3. **Trend tracking** — period-over-period changes (MoM, YoY)
4. **Outlier flagging** — records exceeding 2 standard deviations or 20% variance threshold
5. **Benchmarking** — compare against budget, historical periods, market data
6. **Narrative summary** — headline finding + supporting bullets with implications

## Common Applications
- Rent roll: occupancy trends, rent per SF vs. market, lease expiration concentration
- Expenses: category analysis, per-SF benchmarks, variance from prior year
- Collections: delinquency rate, aging buckets, tenant-level patterns
- Tenant concentration: % of revenue from top 3/5/10 tenants

## Output Format
Lead with the headline finding, follow with 3–5 supporting data points, close with implications or recommended actions. Present structured tables, not raw data dumps.

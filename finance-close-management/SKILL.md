# Finance: Close Management

Manage month-end and quarter-end close processes for industrial real estate funds. Role: Controller. Frequency: Monthly/Quarterly.

## Close Timeline

**Pre-Close (Days -3 to 0)**
- Confirm AP invoice cutoff
- Confirm cash receipts posted
- Review AR aging
- Obtain bank statements
- Lock transactions in prior period

**Transaction Close (Days 1–3)**
- Post recurring journal entries
- Record accruals
- Clear intercompany
- Complete bank reconciliations
- Tie out sub-ledgers
- Assign owners and due dates for each task

**Review & Reporting (Days 4–7)**
- Review trial balance
- Variance analysis vs. prior period and budget
- Draft financial statements
- Management approval

**Quarter-End Add-Ons**
- Debt compliance certificates
- Capital account reconciliation
- Preferred return calculations
- LP reporting package
- Tax provisions

## Status Tracking Format
- **COMPLETE** — task finished
- **IN PROGRESS** — ongoing, include ETA
- **BLOCKED** — awaiting external input, identify blocker

## Escalation Triggers
Notify management if:
- Close delayed beyond 2 business days
- Unexplained variance > $10K
- Property manager data missing at Day 3

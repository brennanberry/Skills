# Finance: Financial Statements

Generate and analyze financial statements for industrial real estate fund entities and property LLCs. Use for LP reporting, lender packages, and internal management. Role: Controller/Fund Manager. Frequency: Monthly.

## Statement Types
- P&L (Income Statement) — property and fund level
- Balance Sheet — property and fund level
- Cash Flow Statement — direct or indirect method

## Analysis Framework
- Period-over-period: current vs. prior month, current vs. prior year
- Budget-to-actual variance tracking
- Financial ratios: NOI margin, expense ratio, DSCR

## Standard Output
Each statement includes:
- Comparative columns (current period, prior period, budget)
- Ratio analysis section
- Management commentary explaining top 3–5 variances

## Reporting Standards
- GAAP basis with comparative periods
- Footnotes for: significant transactions, accounting changes, related-party dealings
- Preparation: Controller → Review: Fund Manager → Distribution: LP/Lender/IC

## Audience Variants
- **LP reports**: Focus on NOI, distributions, IRR, occupancy
- **Lender packages**: Emphasize DSCR, occupancy, covenant compliance
- **Internal IC**: Full detail with budget variance and forward outlook

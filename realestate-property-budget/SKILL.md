# Real Estate: Property Budget

Develop comprehensive annual budgets for industrial real estate assets. Use for annual budget preparation (typically Q3–Q4 for following year), mid-year reforecasting, and variance analysis.

## Budget Sections

**Revenue Build**
- Tenant-by-tenant rents, escalations, and vacancy assumptions → Effective Gross Income

**Operating Expenses**
- Line-item tracking: taxes, insurance, utilities, maintenance, management fees
- Compare against prior-year actuals

**Capital Budget**
- Project-level: lifecycle replacements, tenant improvements, value-add initiatives
- Include cost and timing for each project

**Debt Service**
- Annual principal and interest with maturity flagging

## Assumptions Page (Required)
Document separately:
- Occupancy rate assumptions with justification
- Rent growth rationale
- Expense escalation factors
- Reserve methodology

## Use Cases
- Annual budget preparation (Q3–Q4)
- Mid-year reforecast
- Budget vs. actual variance analysis for asset reviews and LP reporting
